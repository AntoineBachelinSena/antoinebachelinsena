STANDARDS APPLIED

Four non-negotiable requirements

It is the discipline of production — more than the subject matter — that constitutes the difference. These are verifiable by any reader of any deliverable.

STANDARD 01

Primacy of primary sources

Secondary sources orient the search. They never establish a fact.

STANDARD 02

Explicit status for every assertion

Established fact · Inference · Hypothesis · Rumour discarded. Marked in the text, never left to the reader to guess.

STANDARD 03

Declared confidence level

High · Medium · Low · Insufficient — always with the reason for the rating.

STANDARD 04

Statement of what cannot be established

What remains out of reach is named, never concealed. This shortens conclusions. It also makes them defensible.

A deliverable meeting these standards can be filed in a case record, cited before a committee, or put to a third party. It is the discipline of production, not the subject matter, that carries the difference.

ABOUT

Antoine Bachelin Sena is a French structural risk analyst, resident in São Paulo since 2011. He works on the Guiana Shield and the South Atlantic, where gold chains, illicit financial flows, critical-resource dependencies and four separate legal orders overlap without ever coinciding.

His analysis sits at an intersection few occupy: a European reading of sovereignty and border integrity; direct institutional access in Brazil and working fluency in Portuguese, Spanish, French and English; literacy in criminal economies and the financial layer beneath them; and the industrial-policy dimension of critical minerals. The object of study is what falls between jurisdictions — and what no single administration is mandated to see whole.

He has published in CEBRI-Journal (Brazilian Center for International Relations) and with CARI (Consejo Argentino para las Relaciones Internacionales), with further work under peer review.

He is Founder & Director of Strategy of the Institut Atlantique Sud.

Working languages: French · Portuguese · Spanish · English. ORCID 0009-0004-8640-3130.
