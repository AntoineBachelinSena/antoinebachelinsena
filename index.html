PROPRIETARY FRAMEWORKS

Analytical tools for high-uncertainty environments

Three original frameworks designed to translate complex hybrid dynamics into structured, decision-oriented insight and actionable recommendations.

CAD™

Critical Alignment Dynamics

Analyses how political, economic and criminal alignments shift under external pressure and internal fragmentation. Identifies emerging coalitions, dependency patterns and points of leverage in contested territories.

Output example: Alignment map / Influence coalition matrix

SRI™

Strategic Resilience Index

Assesses the capacity of states, territories or organisations to absorb geopolitical pressure, hybrid interference and strategic dependencies. Highlights institutional strengths, structural vulnerabilities and potential breaking points.

Output example: Resilience scorecard / Multi-axis vulnerability dashboard

IEDM™

Institutional Exposure & Dependency Mapping

Maps actors, networks, financial and logistical flows to reveal hidden exposure and influence channels. Supports identification of concentration risks and structural vulnerabilities often missed by classical assessments.

Output example: Dependency map / Prioritised action matrix (0-3 / 3-6 months)

ABOUT

Antoine Bachelin Sena is a French strategic risk analyst based in São Paulo since 2011, specialising in hybrid threats, strategic dependencies and sovereignty risks across Latin America.

His work focuses particularly on the Guiana Shield, French Guiana and the South Atlantic, where geopolitical competition, criminal networks, critical resources and institutional vulnerabilities converge.

Combining fifteen years of direct field experience in Brazil with a European perspective on sovereignty and state resilience, he analyses how state and non-state actors exploit jurisdictional asymmetries and strategic vulnerabilities.

He is the Founder & Director of Strategy of the Institut Atlantique Sud, developing research and advisory capabilities on French Guiana as Europe’s strategic frontier in the Amazon Basin.
